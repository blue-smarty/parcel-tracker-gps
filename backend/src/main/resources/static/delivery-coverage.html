Manage service areas

Admin settings Dispatch workspace

Set where deliveries are accepted and assign couriers to drawn areas. Changes are audited and do not affect existing deliveries.

Both pickup and delivery addresses must be inside selected coverage. Address and boundary verification must be available when restricted.

Coverage areas are combined: an address can be in any selected state, council or drawn area. Council boundaries are ABS statistical approximations, not legal boundaries.

Use Ctrl or Command to select multiple options. Draw an area on the map by placing at least three boundary points, then choose Finish. The first drawn area matching a new delivery determines its courier; state and council selections do not assign couriers.
